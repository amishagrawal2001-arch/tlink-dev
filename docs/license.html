License & activation guide

Everything about licensing Tlink

Sign in, activate online or offline, and recover when things break — all without leaving the app. The pill in the bottom-right is the single source of truth for license state, and we never block the app over a billing hiccup.

Quickstart

Your first launch

Install Tlink and you’re on a 30-day local trial from the moment it opens. Here’s the path from trial to signed-in paid plan.

1

Install & open

The Sign in to activate modal opens automatically. The pill in the bottom-right shows Trial: 30d.

2

Try before you buy

Press Escape to dismiss the modal. You have the full 30-day trial. The pill keeps counting down.

3

Click Upgrade

When you’re ready, click the green Upgrade pill next to the trial countdown. Pricing opens in your browser.

4

Pay & receive email

After checkout you get a sign-in email with the email + password for your account.

5

Sign in

Click the trial pill → Sign in to activate → enter credentials. Pill flips to Individual or Team. Done.

Dock pill reference

One pill, every state

The pill in the bottom-right of the window shows your current license state at a glance. Hover any pill for a tooltip with the exact detail; click opens the matching action.

Pill Means Click to
Trial: Nd Local 30-day trial, N days remaining. App is fully functional. Open account menu (Sign in, License settings).
Upgrade Shown next to the trial pill. Opens the pricing / checkout page. Open the pricing page in your browser.
Sign in You haven’t signed in yet (or you signed out) and aren’t on a trial. Open the Sign-in modal.
Individual Signed in on a paid solo plan. Open account menu.
Team Signed in on a team plan — a seat allocated by your admin. Open account menu.
Trial Server-side trial (a purchased trial seat, not the local 30-day one). Open account menu.
Reactivate License expired, invalid, or seat revoked. App drops into read-only mode — existing tabs keep working, new SSH / RDP / collector / gNMI sessions are blocked. Open the activation modal to fix.
Why doesn’t Tlink block the app on expiry? Paying users whose card just failed shouldn’t get the same hammer as someone who never paid. We keep your existing tabs viewable and let you look at your work while you fix billing — the Reactivate pill stays visible until you’re back.
Activate

Two activation paths

Most users sign in with email + password. Air-gapped or restricted networks use the offline-code path — your admin mints a device-bound code so the app never touches the license server.

Standard

Online activation

  1. 1Click the Sign in or Reactivate pill in the bottom-right.
  2. 2Type your account email & password.
  3. 3Click Sign in. The modal closes and the pill flips to your plan name.
Tokens are saved to your OS keychain (macOS Keychain · Windows Credential Manager · Linux libsecret) so you stay signed in across restarts. Tlink refreshes the session automatically every hour.
Air-gapped / restricted networks

Offline activation

  1. 1Click the Sign in pill to open the modal.
  2. 2Click Have an activation code? at the bottom — the modal flips to offline mode.
  3. 3Copy your device fingerprint (a short hash). Send it to your admin.
  4. 4Admin mints an offline code bound to that fingerprint and sends it back. The code starts with eyJ…
  5. 5Paste the code into Activation code → click Activate. Pill flips to your plan name.
Offline codes carry their own expiry (set by your admin at mint time). When the code expires you’ll get a Reactivate pill; repeat the process with a fresh code.
Manage

Settings → License

The full license management page lives at Settings → License (sidebar gear icon → License). Everything you can do from the dock pill, plus the full picture.

Account

Your signed-in email, plan name, device fingerprint — everything that identifies this install.

Refresh from server

Force a heartbeat if the pill shows stale state. Useful after your admin changes your plan or adds you to a team.

Session details

Last server contact, heartbeat count, round-trip time, offline grace status — everything about the client/server link.

Server URL

Override the license server endpoint if your organization runs a self-hosted deployment (Team plan).

Sign out of this device

Frees the seat on the server. You’ll land in the Sign-in state — not back to the trial (one trial per device).

Clear local session

Nuclear option, available from the activation modal’s footer. Wipes keychain tokens & local state — use when the app is wedged.

Troubleshoot

When things go wrong

Tlink prefers to keep working rather than block you. Every failure mode is a Reactivate pill plus read-only mode — existing tabs stay viewable, only new sessions are blocked.

R Trial expired, haven’t paid

Read-only mode. Click the Reactivate pill to sign in or activate — nothing you had open goes away.

R Paid license expired (card declined)

Same Reactivate pill, same read-only mode. Fix billing in your account; back in Tlink click Settings → License → Refresh from server and you’re back.

T Seat revoked by admin

Reactivate pill with tooltip “Your seat was revoked by your admin”. Contact your admin.

T Device limit reached

You’ve installed on more devices than your plan allows. Sign out of an older device from Settings → License → Sign out of this device on that machine, or do it from your account web page.

O License server unreachable

You’ll see an Offline banner briefly. Tlink grants a 48-hour offline grace — a flaky VPN or airport Wi-Fi dropout won’t lock you out.

After 48 h without server contact the Reactivate pill appears.

O Offline code expired

Reactivate pill. Request a fresh offline code from your admin and repeat the offline activation steps.

In every failure mode the activation dialog is non-blocking. You can close it, keep looking at existing tabs, and open it again whenever you’re ready. The Clear local session and start over link in the modal footer is the nuclear option: wipes keychain tokens and local trial state, drops you back to the first-launch flow.
Team admin

Running Tlink for a team

Team plans ship with centrally managed seats, offline-code minting for restricted networks, and an optional self-hostable license server.

Invite by email

Add team members from the Team dashboard. They get a sign-in email and activate like any Individual plan.

Mint offline codes

Generate device-bound codes for team members on air-gapped networks. Same offline-activation flow from the user side.

Admin panel in-app

Settings → License → Open admin panel (admins only). Seat usage, pending invites, device management.

Self-hosted server

Point Settings → License → Server URL at your own deployment. Keeps license traffic on your network.

FAQ

Common questions

I don’t want to sign up for an account to try Tlink.

You don’t have to. The 30-day local trial starts automatically on first launch. The sign-in modal is skippable with Escape.

Do I need to be online for Tlink to work?

Only to activate. After sign-in Tlink heartbeats every hour but tolerates up to 48 hours offline before asking you to reconnect. Offline-activated installs don’t heartbeat at all until their code expires.

Can I use one license on multiple devices?

Individual plan: 2 devices concurrent. Team plan: configured per-seat by your admin (typically 1-3 devices per seat).

I signed out and now I’m stuck in “Sign in” state — can I get my trial back?

The 30-day trial is per-device. Once consumed you need a paid plan or an admin-minted offline code. Clear local session and start over in the activation modal wipes device state but does not reset the trial clock.

My card failed and the app went into read-only mode — did I lose my work?

No. All open tabs stay viewable. SSH / RDP sessions you had open keep running. You just can’t open new profile sessions until you reactivate. Fix billing, click Refresh from server in Settings → License, and the pill flips back to your plan name.

I just upgraded from Individual to Team / my admin added me to a team — my pill still shows the old plan.

Settings → License → Refresh from server. Takes 1-2 seconds. If still wrong after a minute, Sign out and Sign back in.

Still stuck?

Hit a case this guide doesn’t cover? Open an issue with your device fingerprint (from Settings → License) and the pill state you’re seeing — we’ll get back to you.
